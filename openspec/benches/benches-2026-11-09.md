# Benchmarks, 2026-11-09: the KV storage baseline

The commit path's throughput and latency through the three role binaries, and the in-process ceiling beside them, at the end of
change `batch-commits-through-the-roles` group 7. The store is fjall, an LSM tree. Roadmap stage 6.5 replaces it with the tile-based
storage engine; its benchmark is run the same way, on the same layout, and compared against these tables.

## What ran

- **Commit:** `6753d3ad` (`batch-commits-through-the-roles: 7.3 and 7.4 measured; 7.5 the owner answers checks while it syncs`).
  The comparison rows marked *group 6* ran the storage and frontend binaries of `152f1aa0`, the change before group 7.
- **Build:** `bazel build -c opt --platforms=@rules_rs//rs/platforms:aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu` of
  `//ultragraphdb/ultragraphdb_config`, `//ultragraphdb/ultragraphdb_frontend`, `//ultragraphdb/ultragraphdb_storage`,

  `//ultragraphdb_tool/ultragraphdb_tool_bench:wal_bench`, and for the profile `//ultragraphdb/ultragraphdb_storage:ultragraphdb_storage_profile`
  (symbols, frame pointers). The binaries are glibc, run directly in the machines below, without images.

  VM; `container` gives a machine one CPU more than `--cpus` asks for.

- **The clients,** on `ugdb-c`: `N` processes of `client_bench`, run together, the connections split evenly (1 process up to 64
  connections, 2 of 128 for 256, 4 of 256 for 1,024), each a 20 s timed run after its logins:

```sh
client_bench --address <F>:7080 --password-file /m7/keys/bootstrap.secret --op direct \
  --relations <R> --connections <C> --partitions <P> --seconds 20
```

  Each commit is a DirectWrite through a session (`--session` is on by default): `R` new nodes, each with an edge to one of 1,000
  hubs and a string. A connection has one commit in flight. The commits a second are the processes' sum; the latencies the median
  process's, from the call to its answer; the mean is weighted by commits.
- **The in-process ceiling,** on `ugdb-s`, as 65532, no other process running:

```sh
wal_bench --dir /var/tmp/m7/wal --partitions <P> --rate 50000 --relations <R> --seconds 20 --cross-percent 0
```

  `wal_bench` writes the same store, log and commit step in one process with no roles: 64 hubs a partition, 10 relation types.
- **Method:** three runs a point, medians with the range in parentheses. The comparison of group 6 against HEAD interleaves the two
  run by run. The share of in-process is relations a second through the binaries over `wal_bench`'s at the same partitions and
  relations a commit. Commits a sync is storage's `commit phases` log: commits over log syncs, summed over its partitions. No run
  refused a commit.

## The storage commit thread

`ultragraphdb_storage_profile` in place of storage, one partition, 16 clients of one process, `perf record -e cpu-clock:u -F 999 -g
--user-callchains -p <storage>` as 65532 for 10 s of a 25 s run (`perf_event_paranoid` is 2, so user space only), stacks demangled
with `c++filt`. Each sample of the partition's thread goes to the innermost phase on its stack: validation (`resolver`,
`conflict_history`), the log (`ultragraphdb_transactions_log`), apply (`ChangesetCore`'s round write and apply), execution
(`ultragraphdb_transactions_txn`, `store_apply`'s `execution` and `changeset_write`), the commit step's decision; the store is every
sample with fjall, `lsm_tree` or `ultragraphdb_store_kv` on its stack. One run each.

| Relations a commit | Commits/s | Relations/s | Thread busy | Thread µs a relation | Execution | Apply | Decision | Validation | Store on the thread, µs a relation | of it reads | writes | lz4 | fjall's workers, µs a relation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8,958 | 8,958 | 42% | 46.4 | 32% | 43% | 7% | 0.8% | 20.2 | 5.0 | 14.5 | 0.0 | 9.1 |
| 10 | 2,122 | 21,220 | 69% | 32.4 | 38% | 52% | 4% | 0.2% | 17.5 | 3.7 | 13.3 | 5.2 | 24.9 |
| 50 | 494 | 24,700 | 75% | 30.5 | 40% | 53% | 3% | 0.0% | 16.5 | 3.7 | 12.5 | 6.0 | 26.5 |

The commit thread fits 30.4 µs a relation and 16.0 µs a commit. The store holds 16.5 to 20 µs of each relation on the thread, writes
most of it, and its workers, flushing and compacting, spend 25 to 27 µs more a relation on threads of their own at 10 and 50
relations a commit: about 42 to 44 µs of CPU a relation in the KV store in all. A relation of this shape writes 9.1 store versions:
2.0 forward, 2.0 reverse, 1.0 subject-predicates, 1.1 object-predicates, 2.0 statistics and 1.0 referrers (counted in
`ultragraphdb_store_apply`'s harness, `batch-commits-through-the-roles` notes, 7.4). Validation, what strict serializability costs,
is 0.8% or less.
